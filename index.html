milConnect will be unavailable for scheduled maintenance on October 9, 2026 with an anticipated end on October 11, 2026. We apologize for any inconvenience.

(!) TRICARE Open Season begins November 9 and ends December 8, 2026

To Learn about your options during TRICARE Open Season, please visit TRICARE's Open Season page.

During Open Season, you can use milConnect to enroll in or change your health plan for next year. To get started, sign into milConnect and choose Beneficiary Web Enrollment (BWE) from the Benefits menu.

(!) INCAP Portal Goes Live September 10, 2026

We're excited to announce that the Incapacitated Adult Child Program (INCAP) Portal will officially go live on September 10, 2026.

The INCAP Portal provides eligible sponsors with a secure, user-friendly way to submit and track INCAP claims online. You can also access helpful resources and guidance to support you throughout the process.

Visit our INCAP Portal page to learn more.
